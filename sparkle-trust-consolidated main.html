Help & FAQ

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send money?＋
Go to the Send tab, enter the recipient's name, account number or email, and the amount. Review the summary and tap Confirm to complete the transfer.
How long do transfers take?＋
Domestic transfers are processed instantly. International transfers typically take 1–3 business days depending on the destination country and currency.
Are my funds insured?＋
Yes. Deposits held in your Primary Savings account are insured up to $250,000 under the FDIC. Investment portfolio funds are covered by SIPC up to $500,000.
How do I dispute a transaction?＋
Go to Transaction History, tap the transaction in question, and select "Dispute Transaction." Our fraud team will review and respond within 3–5 business days.
What currencies are supported?＋
We support USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, and 20+ additional currencies. Multi-currency accounts can hold and exchange all supported currencies.
How do I increase my transfer limit?＋
Contact our support team or visit a branch with a valid government-issued ID. Premium account holders can request an increase online via the Security settings.
